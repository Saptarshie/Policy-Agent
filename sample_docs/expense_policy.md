# Northwind Robotics — Travel & Expense Policy

**Policy Number:** HR-EXP-2024-003
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2024
**Applies To:** All employees of Northwind Robotics

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines which expenses Northwind Robotics reimburses and the rules for spending, approving, and claiming them. It applies to all employees and covers company-paid travel, client entertainment, and remote-work equipment and services. To be reimbursable, an expense must be ordinary, reasonable, and directly connected to company business. Where this policy sets a limit, the limit is a cap and not a target; managers may always approve less, and amounts above a cap require a documented exception.

## 2. Reimbursable Categories

The following recurring remote-work categories are reimbursed monthly, up to the stated limits.

| Category | Monthly Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home internet | $60 | Must be the employee's primary connection |
| Mobile phone | $45 | One line per employee |
| Coworking space | $200 | Pre-approval required before signing a contract |
| Wellness stipend | $40 | Gym, yoga, and fitness class memberships only |
| Professional books | $50 | Work-related titles |
| Software subscriptions | $30 per tool | Manager approval required |

Each category requires an itemized receipt and, where noted, prior written approval. Recurring stipends are claimed monthly and do not roll over; any amount not claimed in a month is forfeited. Amounts above a monthly limit are not reimbursed without a documented exception approved by the employee's Director.

## 3. Non-Reimbursable Items

The following items are not reimbursable under any circumstances:

- Home gym and exercise equipment, including treadmills, weights, and stationary bikes
- Furniture, including desks, chairs, and shelving
- Personal streaming services (video, music, and gaming)
- Childcare and eldercare
- Commuting between home and the office
- Alcohol purchased for personal consumption
- Traffic fines and parking violations

The wellness stipend covers memberships and class fees only. Fitness equipment purchases are excluded in all cases, including "smart" or connected fitness equipment, regardless of whether the vendor markets the purchase as a subscription.

## 4. Business Travel

Flights must be booked in economy class for all trips with a total flight time under 6 hours. Business class bookings require prior Director approval and are permitted only for flights of 6 hours or more. All travel must be booked through the company travel portal; bookings made outside the portal are reimbursed only with Director approval. The per diem for meals and incidental expenses is $60 per day for domestic travel and $75 per day for international travel, paid without receipts. Hotel reimbursement is capped at $180 per night in domestic locations and $220 per night in international tier-1 cities; the list of tier-1 cities is maintained in the travel portal. Ground transportation, including airport transfers and rideshare between hotels and work sites, is reimbursed at actual cost with receipts.

## 5. Client Entertainment

Client entertainment expenses are reimbursed up to a maximum of $150 per person per event, excluding taxes and gratuity. All client entertainment requires prior VP approval, and the business purpose and attendee list must be recorded on the expense report. Alcohol-only events are not permitted, and alcohol may never be the primary purpose of a reimbursed gathering.

## 6. Expense Submission

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date through Expensify. Itemized receipts are required for any single expense above $25. Approvals follow the company spending matrix: direct managers approve expenses under $500, Directors approve expenses from $500 to $2,000, and the VP approves expenses above $2,000. Expenses incurred in a foreign currency are converted at the corporate card exchange rate on the transaction date. Submissions more than 60 days after the expense date require a written justification and are paid at Finance's discretion.

## 7. Violations

Managers and Finance review expense reports for compliance with this policy. Repeated violations result in disciplinary action, up to and including loss of reimbursement privileges. Submitting fraudulent claims, including fabricated receipts, inflated amounts, or misstated business purposes, is grounds for immediate termination and recovery of the amounts paid.
