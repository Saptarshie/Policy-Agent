# Northwind Robotics — Health Plans (2025 Plan Year)

**Policy Number:** HR-BEN-2025-002
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Plan Year:** January 1 – December 31, 2025
**Insurer:** Meridian Health Assurance — Policy #MHA-2025-NR

## 1. Plan Tiers

Northwind Robotics offers group health coverage to employees through Meridian Health Assurance. Employees choose one of three tiers — Standard, Plus, or Premium — at enrollment, and may change tiers only during annual open enrollment or after a qualifying life event. The company pays 80% of the monthly premium for the Standard tier, 75% for the Plus tier, and 70% for the Premium tier; the employee pays the remaining share through payroll deduction. Premium rates for each tier are published in the HRMS before each open enrollment period.

The table below summarizes the key benefits of each tier. All amounts are in US dollars and apply to in-network care unless stated otherwise.

Once the annual out-of-pocket maximum is reached, the plan pays 100% of covered in-network services for the remainder of the plan year.

## 3. Dental Coverage Details

Dental implants are classified by Meridian Health Assurance as major restorative services. The Standard tier excludes major restorative services entirely, so dental implants are not covered under the Standard tier under any circumstances. The Plus and Premium tiers cover dental implants after a 12-month waiting period from the plan enrollment date. Pre-authorization from the insurer is required before scheduling any implant procedure; treatment begun without pre-authorization may be denied. Bone grafting, which frequently accompanies implant procedures, is covered only under the Premium tier at 50% coinsurance and is not covered under Standard or Plus.

## 4. Eligibility and Enrollment

All full-time employees are eligible for health coverage after 30 days of continuous employment, with coverage effective the first of the month following completion of that period. Employees may enroll a spouse or domestic partner and eligible dependents at an additional premium paid through payroll deduction. Annual open enrollment is held each November, with elections effective the following January 1. Outside of open enrollment, employees may change tiers or update dependents only after a qualifying life event, such as marriage, the birth or adoption of a child, or the loss of other coverage. Life event changes must be requested within 30 days of the event.

## 5. Claims Process

Claims for reimbursement must be submitted within 90 days of the date of service. Claims are filed through the Meridian member portal or the HRMS claims form, and employees are reimbursed within 15 business days of submitting complete documentation. Incomplete claims are returned with a request for the missing information, and the original 90-day submission window continues to apply from the date of service. Employees are responsible for confirming that a provider is in-network before receiving treatment.

## 6. Exclusions

The following services are excluded from coverage under all tiers: cosmetic surgery and purely cosmetic dental procedures; experimental or investigational treatments that have not been approved by the insurer; and over-the-counter supplements, vitamins, and wellness products. Services that are not listed as covered in the Schedule of Benefits are also excluded. This list is a summary only; the complete exclusions are defined in the Meridian Health Assurance master policy documents.

## 7. Governance

This document is owned by the HR Benefits Committee, which meets quarterly to review plan utilization, cost trends, and employee feedback. Coverage is administered by Meridian Health Assurance under master policy number MHA-2025-NR. Proposed changes to plan design are reviewed by the Benefits Committee and require CFO approval before the start of a plan year. Questions about claims, coverage, or enrollment should be directed to benefits@northwindrobotics.com.
