# Northwind Robotics — Leave Policy

**Policy Number:** HR-LVE-2024-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Version:** 1.2
**Owner:** HR Director

## 1. Overview

Northwind Robotics provides paid leave to support the health, well-being, and work-life balance of every full-time employee. This policy defines the available leave types, annual entitlements, accrual mechanics, carry-forward rules, and the application procedure. It applies to all regular full-time employees in the United States.

Employees on probation (the first 6 months of employment) accrue leave at the standard rates described in Section 2. However, during probation an employee may not take more than 1 casual leave day per month. This monthly limit does not apply to sick leave or parental leave.

## 2. Leave Types and Annual Entitlements

The table below summarizes the leave types available to full-time employees and how each accrues.

| Leave Type | Annual Entitlement | Accrual | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Casual Leave | 12 days | 1 day per month | Personal matters, family obligations, and short absences |
| Sick Leave | 12 days | 1 day per month | Medical certificate required for 3 or more consecutive sick days |
| Earned/Privilege Leave | 18 days | 1.5 days per month | Up to 5 unused days may be encashed at year end |
| Parental Leave | 26 weeks (primary caregiver) / 4 weeks (secondary caregiver) | Granted in full when leave begins | Requires 12 months of continuous service |

Entitlements are expressed in working days, prorated for approved part-time arrangements.

## 3. Casual Leave

Casual Leave is the default paid leave type for absences that are not illness-related. It is intended for planned personal matters, family obligations, religious observances not covered by the holiday calendar, and short unforeseen absences. It should not be used to extend a public holiday or weekend without the manager's approval, and may not be combined with Leave Without Pay in a single absence.

## 3.1 Accrual and Usage

Casual Leave accrues at 1 day per completed month of service, up to the annual entitlement of 12 days per calendar year. New employees begin accruing from their start date, and the balance is visible in the HRMS at the start of each month. Employees may use casual leave as soon as it has accrued; advancing usage against future accrual is not permitted. Probationary employees remain limited to 1 casual leave day per month, as described in Section 1.

## 3.2 Carry-Forward

Unused casual leave may be carried forward into the next calendar year, capped at 6 days. Any unused balance above 6 days lapses permanently on December 31 and cannot be recovered, encashed, or transferred to another leave type. The carry-forward is applied automatically in the first payroll cycle of January, so no action is required from employees or managers. Earned/Privilege Leave carries forward under separate rules and is not affected by the 6-day casual leave cap.

## 3.3 Application Procedure

All casual leave requests must be submitted through the HRMS portal; requests by email or chat are not formally submitted. Planned casual leave requires a minimum of 2 working days advance notice before the requested start date. Managers must approve or reject a request within 3 working days of submission; if no decision is recorded in that time, the request automatically escalates to the next-level manager. Unplanned casual leave must be reported to the manager before 10:00 AM local time on the day of absence and regularized in the HRMS within 2 working days.

## 4. Sick Leave

Sick Leave is available for an employee's own illness, injury, or medical appointments. A medical certificate from a licensed physician is required for any absence of 3 or more consecutive sick days and must be uploaded to the HRMS within 5 working days of returning to work. Sick Leave cannot be combined with Casual Leave for the same absence, and managers may request documentation for repeated single-day absences. Unused sick leave does not carry forward to the next calendar year.

## 5. Parental Leave

Parental Leave supports employees welcoming a child through birth, adoption, or foster placement. To be eligible, an employee must have completed 12 months of continuous service with Northwind Robotics as of the intended leave start date. Eligible employees receive 26 weeks of parental leave as the primary caregiver or 4 weeks as the secondary caregiver. Leave should be requested at least 30 days in advance where practicable. Benefits continue during parental leave, and employees return to the same or an equivalent role.

## 6. Leave Without Pay

Leave Without Pay (LWOP) may be granted when an employee has exhausted all paid leave balances. LWOP is capped at 90 days in any rolling 12-month period and requires written approval from the employee's Vice President in addition to manager approval. Requests must be submitted through the HRMS at least 10 working days in advance, except in documented emergencies. Prolonged LWOP may affect benefits continuation and accrual seniority; HR confirms any effects in writing beforehand.

## 7. Holidays

Northwind Robotics observes 10 public holidays per year, as published in the annual holiday calendar, including New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Every employee also receives 2 floating holidays per calendar year, usable for religious observances, cultural events, or personal milestones, subject to manager approval. Unused floating holidays expire on December 31.

## 8. Policy Governance

This policy is owned by the HR Director, who is responsible for its content, interpretation, and maintenance. It is reviewed annually and updated as business or regulatory needs require. Exceptions to any provision require written approval from both the CTO and HR and are documented in the employee's HRMS record. Questions should be directed to People Operations at hr@northwindrobotics.com.
