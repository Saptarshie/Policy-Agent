ACME CORPORATION
Remote Work Policy (v1.0)

Effective date: 2024-01-01
Owner: People Operations

1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules under which employees of ACME Corporation may work remotely. It applies to all full-time employees in engineering, design, and support functions. Contractors and interns are covered by separate agreements.

2. Remote Work Allowance

Employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per calendar week. The remaining working days must be spent at the assigned office location. A remote work arrangement must be approved in writing by the employee's direct manager before it starts.

3. Notice Period

Employees must give at least 15 days written notice to their manager before changing or terminating an approved remote work arrangement.

4. Eligibility

Employees are eligible to apply for remote work after completing 6 months of continuous employment. Employees on a performance improvement plan are not eligible.

5. Equipment and Security

The company provides a laptop and a security token for remote work. Employees must connect through the corporate VPN whenever they access company systems from outside the office. Personal printers may not be used for company documents.

6. Core Hours

Remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 16:00 local time on all working days. Meetings may be scheduled inside this window without special accommodation.
