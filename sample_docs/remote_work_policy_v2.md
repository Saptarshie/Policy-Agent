# ACME Hybrid Work Policy (v2.0)

_Effective: 2025-06-01. Owner: People Operations._

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines ACME Corporation's hybrid work rules for all full-time employees in every function.

## 2. Office Attendance

Employees must work from their assigned office at least 4 days per calendar week. Remote work is limited to a maximum of 1 day per week and requires written approval from the direct manager for each remote day.

## 3. Notice Period

Employees must submit any change to their working-location pattern at least 30 days in advance through the HR portal. Verbal agreements are not valid.

## 4. Eligibility

Hybrid arrangements are available only to employees who have completed 12 months of continuous employment and who hold a performance rating of "meets expectations" or higher in the most recent review cycle.

## 5. Equipment

The company provides a laptop, a security token, and a monitor for home use. All remote access must go through the corporate VPN with multi-factor authentication. Personal printers are prohibited for company documents.

## 6. Core Hours

All employees, including those working remotely on an approved day, must be reachable between 09:00 and 17:00 local time on all working days.
