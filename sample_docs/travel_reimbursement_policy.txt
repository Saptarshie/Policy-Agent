ACME CORPORATION
Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

Effective date: 2024-03-01

1. Scope

This policy covers travel booking and expense reimbursement for all employees travelling on company business. It does not cover commuting expenses.

2. Booking Rules

All business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Flights must be economy class for trips under six hours. Hotel bookings are capped at 180 USD per night for domestic travel.

3. Receipts and Submission

Employees must submit all travel expense receipts within 30 days of completing the trip. Claims submitted through the corporate portal are processed within 10 business days. Missing receipts require a signed missing-receipt declaration.

4. Approval

Trips exceeding 3 days or 1500 USD in total estimated cost require director approval before booking. International travel always requires director approval regardless of duration or cost.

5. Per Diem

The per diem for domestic travel is 60 USD per day. No receipts are required for per diem claims. Alcoholic beverages are never reimbursable.

6. Non-Reimbursable Items

Traffic fines, airline lounge memberships, in-flight entertainment, and mini-bar charges are not reimbursable under any circumstances.
