# Leave Policy Amendment — 2025 (Policy CL-2025-07)

**Effective Date:** July 1, 2025
**Amends:** Northwind Robotics Leave Policy (HR-LVE-2024-001), Sections 3.2 and 3.3

This amendment amends Section 3.2 (Carry-Forward) of the Northwind Robotics Leave Policy. Effective July 1, 2025, the carry-forward cap for unused casual leave is increased from 6 days to 15 days. Unused casual leave may be carried forward into the next calendar year up to a maximum of 15 days; any balance above 15 days lapses on December 31. The carry-forward continues to be applied automatically in the first payroll cycle of January.

This amendment also amends Section 3.3 (Application Procedure) of the Leave Policy. Effective July 1, 2025, the minimum advance notice for planned casual leave is reduced from 2 working days to 1 working day. All other requirements of Section 3.3 remain unchanged.

## Rationale

The changes were adopted as a retention measure following the 2025 employee engagement survey, in which respondents ranked leave flexibility among the highest-impact improvements. Increasing the carry-forward cap and shortening the notice period give employees greater flexibility to plan time off while preserving managers' ability to schedule coverage.

## Scope

This amendment applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Robotics, including employees currently on probation. It is administered by the HR Director together with the underlying Leave Policy.
