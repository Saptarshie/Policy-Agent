# Northstar Dynamics Business Travel, Relocation, and Mobility Policy

**Policy ID:** TRV-MOB-2026  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective date:** 1 June 2026  
**Policy owner:** Global Mobility and Finance Operations

> This fictional policy governs authorized business travel, temporary assignments, relocation benefits, and related claims.

## 1. Scope and authorization

Business travel must have a documented business purpose and manager approval before booking. International travel also requires department-head approval. Travelers must use the company's designated booking channel unless the travel desk documents that the required itinerary is unavailable.

## 2. Booking class

| Journey | Standard entitlement | Exception authority |
|---|---|---|
| Flight under 6 hours | Economy | Department head for documented medical accommodation |
| Flight 6–10 hours | Economy with extra-legroom seat | Department head |
| Flight over 10 hours | Premium economy | Business head |
| Train journey under 5 hours | AC Chair Car | Department head |
| Train journey 5 hours or more | AC 2-tier | Department head |

Duration means scheduled nonstop flight time for the booked segment, not total door-to-door travel. Business class is permitted only for an approved medical accommodation or when no lower cabin is available for urgent travel and the business head approves in writing.

## 3. Hotels

Nightly hotel caps, excluding taxes, are:

| City category | Domestic cap | International cap |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tier A / high cost | ₹9,000 | USD 220 |
| Tier B | ₹6,500 | USD 170 |
| Other | ₹5,000 | USD 130 |

A traveler may exceed the cap by up to 20% without additional approval when the designated booking tool shows no compliant hotel within 5 km of the work site. A larger exception requires department-head approval before booking. Laundry is reimbursable after 5 consecutive hotel nights, up to ₹1,500 per trip.

## 4. Meals and incidentals

Domestic travel uses actual meal cost with receipts, capped at ₹1,800 per full travel day and ₹900 for a partial day. International travel uses the destination per diem shown by the booking tool. Meals provided by a hotel, conference, airline, or host must be deducted: breakfast 20%, lunch 30%, dinner 40% of the daily allowance.

Alcohol is not reimbursable. Tips are reimbursable where customary, up to 10% of the eligible service cost.

## 5. Ground transport

Reasonable taxi, public transport, airport transfer, toll, and parking costs are reimbursable. Rental cars require pre-approval from the travel desk. Fines, traffic violations, valet upgrades, and damage caused by unauthorized drivers are not reimbursable.

Mileage for use of a personal car on approved business travel is reimbursed at ₹14 per kilometre. Normal travel between home and the employee's assigned office is commuting and is not reimbursable.

## 6. Travel insurance and medical events

Employees on authorized business travel are covered by the corporate travel-insurance policy for emergency medical treatment, evacuation, trip interruption, and lost checked baggage, subject to insurer terms. Routine medical treatment should use the employee health plan.

Travel vaccinations are covered by the health plan only when the business trip is authorized. If a vaccine must be obtained before trip approval due to lead time, written pre-approval from both the manager and travel desk is required for reimbursement.

## 7. Combining business and personal travel

Employees may add personal days to a business trip with manager approval. The company pays only the lower of: (a) the business-only itinerary quoted at approval time, or (b) the actual combined itinerary. Personal lodging, meals, insurance extensions, visa costs for companions, and itinerary-change fees caused by personal plans are the employee's responsibility.

A companion's costs are not reimbursable unless the companion is required as part of an approved medical accommodation.

## 8. Expense submission

Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 calendar days after trip completion, regardless of the individual transaction dates. Claims submitted 31–60 days after trip completion require department-head approval. Claims after 60 days are rejected except for a documented system outage or medical emergency.

Foreign-currency expenses are converted using the corporate-card rate where available; otherwise, the expense system's rate for the transaction date applies. Employees should not manually add bank conversion fees unless separately charged and documented.

## 9. Relocation eligibility

A permanent employee asked by the company to move their primary residence at least **75 km** for a new assigned work location is eligible for relocation support. Employee-requested moves are not eligible unless the offer letter or transfer letter states otherwise.

The relocation must begin within 6 months of the transfer effective date. Benefits expire after 9 months unless Global Mobility grants a written extension.

## 10. Relocation benefits

| Benefit | Individual employee | Employee with enrolled dependants |
|---|---:|---:|
| Household-goods shipment | Up to ₹80,000 | Up to ₹1,50,000 |
| Temporary lodging | 14 nights | 21 nights |
| One-way travel to new location | Employee | Employee + enrolled dependants |
| Brokerage for rental home | Up to one month's rent, max ₹40,000 | Up to one month's rent, max ₹60,000 |
| Relocation incidentals | ₹10,000 lump sum | ₹20,000 lump sum |

Temporary lodging above the stated night limit requires Global Mobility approval. Security deposits and home purchases are not reimbursable.

## 11. Relocation repayment

An employee who voluntarily resigns within 12 months of the relocation effective date must repay relocation benefits according to this schedule:

- Resignation within 0–6 completed months: 100% repayment.
- Resignation after 6 but before 12 completed months: 50% repayment.
- Resignation at or after 12 completed months: no repayment.

No repayment is required for involuntary separation without cause, death, long-term disability, or a company-directed second relocation.

## 12. Temporary assignments

A temporary assignment lasts 31–180 calendar days at a location other than the assigned office. The employee's assigned office does not change. Approved lodging and one round trip home every 30 days are covered. Internet provided by temporary lodging is treated as a travel expense; the employee may not also claim a separate remote-work internet reimbursement for the same cost.

Employees remain eligible for health benefits and continue to accrue leave. Location-specific public holidays follow the temporary assignment location only when the assignment letter explicitly says so; otherwise, the HR-assigned location remains controlling.

## 13. Accessibility and caregiver travel

Reasonable travel accommodations require written approval through the Workplace and Expense Policy's accommodation process. An approved accessible seat, required companion, or specialized ground transport is not constrained by ordinary class or ground-transport limits when specifically authorized.

Employees traveling during a partial caregiver-leave period must obtain People Operations approval and may claim business travel only for days designated as working days. Attendance-based meal and commuting allowances remain suspended on caregiver-leave days.

## 14. Safety and disruption

Employees should not travel when a company security advisory prohibits the destination. In an emergency, reasonable safety costs may be incurred without prior approval, but the employee must contact the travel desk as soon as practical. Travelers are never required to share a hotel room.

## 15. Examples

### Example A: Personal extension

A business-only fare is ₹28,000. The combined itinerary with two personal days costs ₹31,500. The company pays ₹28,000, and the employee pays the difference plus all personal lodging and meals.

### Example B: Relocation repayment

An employee with dependants receives ₹1,20,000 in reimbursed relocation benefits and resigns voluntarily after 8 completed months. The repayment rate is 50%, so ₹60,000 is due.

### Example C: Long flight

A scheduled nonstop flight is 11 hours. Premium economy is the standard entitlement. A connection that makes total travel 13 hours does not upgrade a 5-hour segment because each booked segment is assessed by scheduled nonstop duration.

## 16. Exclusions and policy silence

This policy does not provide reimbursement for passports, personal vacation, companion leisure costs, commuting, property purchases, or unapproved immigration advice. When a situation is not addressed, obtain written guidance from the travel desk before committing funds.
