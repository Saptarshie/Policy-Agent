# Northstar Dynamics Workplace, Expense, and Remote Work Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-WR-2026  
**Version:** 4.1  
**Effective date:** 1 April 2026  
**Policy owners:** Finance Operations and People Operations

> This fictional policy defines reimbursable business and remote-work expenses. It does not override health-plan exclusions or travel rules.

## 1. Principles

An expense must be necessary for company work, reasonable in amount, supported by evidence, and submitted on time. Manager approval confirms business purpose but does not make an otherwise ineligible item eligible. Employees should not split a purchase to avoid an approval threshold.

## 2. Standard submission rules

Expense reports must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the transaction, except wellbeing-wallet claims, which follow the 45-day deadline in the Health and Wellbeing Benefits Policy. A valid tax invoice and proof of payment are required for claims above ₹500. Finance may request evidence for any amount.

Claims submitted 31–60 days after the transaction require department-head approval. Claims submitted after 60 days are rejected unless Finance Operations confirms a system outage or documented medical emergency.

## 3. Home-office setup allowance

### 3.1 Eligibility and amount

Permanent employees designated Hybrid or Remote receive a one-time **₹25,000 home-office setup allowance** after completing 30 calendar days of employment. Office-designated employees do not receive this allowance unless an approved accessibility accommodation requires equipment at home.

### 3.2 Eligible items

- Desk, capped at ₹12,000.
- Ergonomic chair, capped at ₹15,000.
- External monitor, capped at ₹18,000.
- Keyboard, mouse, webcam, headset, laptop stand, and task light.
- Surge protector and necessary display or power cables.

The combined reimbursement cannot exceed ₹25,000 even if individual item caps would permit a higher total.

### 3.3 Ineligible items

- Personal computers, tablets, phones, televisions, gaming consoles, and printers.
- Decorations, air conditioners, generators, and household furniture.
- Exercise equipment, including standing treadmills, stationary bicycles, weights, and home-gym machines.
- Installation labor, extended warranties, and subscription services.

A conventional height-adjustable desk is eligible within the desk cap. A treadmill desk is not eligible because exercise equipment remains excluded regardless of claimed ergonomic purpose.

### 3.4 Ownership

Items reimbursed under the setup allowance become the employee's property. No replacement allowance is provided for 36 months, except where an approved accessibility accommodation states otherwise.

## 4. Monthly remote-work support

Hybrid and Remote employees may claim:

| Category | Hybrid | Remote | Evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Broadband internet | Up to ₹1,000/month | Up to ₹1,500/month | Monthly bill + payment proof |
| Mobile data backup | Not covered | Up to ₹300/month | Itemized bill |
| Coworking day pass | Up to ₹1,200/month | Up to ₹2,500/month | Invoice + manager approval |
| Electricity | Not separately reimbursed | Not separately reimbursed | N/A |

Unused monthly limits do not roll over. Employees on continuous paid or unpaid leave for an entire calendar month cannot claim that month's internet or coworking support. A month containing at least one worked day remains eligible, subject to actual cost.

## 5. Meals and local transport

### 5.1 Late-working meal

A meal up to ₹600 is reimbursable when an employee works at least 2 hours beyond their scheduled end time with manager approval. The employee must be working from a company office or approved client site. Employees working from home are not eligible for the late-working meal.

### 5.2 Late-working transport

Company-arranged cab service should be used where available. Otherwise, actual taxi cost is reimbursable for travel from a company office or client site after 9:00 p.m. Public transport is encouraged before 9:00 p.m. Personal vehicle fuel for normal commuting is not reimbursable.

## 6. Learning and certification budget

Permanent employees receive ₹30,000 per fiscal year for role-relevant courses, books, examinations, and professional memberships. Manager pre-approval is required before purchase. Certification retake fees are covered only once per certification per fiscal year. The budget cannot be used for degree tuition, general-interest hobbies, or equipment.

Employees who resign within 90 calendar days after reimbursement of a single learning expense above ₹20,000 must repay 50% of that expense. Involuntary separation without cause and internal transfer are exempt from repayment.

## 7. Accessibility accommodations

Accessibility equipment is not charged against the ₹25,000 setup allowance when approved through the accommodation process. The employee submits a request to People Operations; medical details are reviewed only by the designated occupational-health partner. Approved items may include specialized input devices, screen-reading software, or an ergonomic setup beyond ordinary caps.

A health-plan recommendation does not itself authorize reimbursement. Written accommodation approval is required before purchase.

## 8. Interaction with wellbeing benefits

Fitness memberships and approved wellbeing items must be claimed through the wellbeing wallet, not as business expenses. Personal home-gym equipment is excluded from both the wellbeing wallet and the home-office allowance. An ergonomic assessment prescribed by an occupational therapist may be eligible under the wellbeing wallet, while equipment resulting from an approved accessibility accommodation is handled under Section 7.

The same expense may never be reimbursed by both programmes. When an item could fit more than one programme, Finance Operations assigns the claim to the most specific applicable benefit.

## 9. Remote-work location and security

Employees may work within India from an address recorded in the HR portal. Working from another state for more than 30 consecutive calendar days requires manager and People Operations approval because payroll and compliance obligations may change. Working outside India requires advance written approval from Legal, Information Security, and People Operations.

Company data must be accessed using approved devices and security controls. Public computers are prohibited. Confidential calls must not be taken where unauthorized persons can hear them.

## 10. Equipment failure

Company-issued laptop failures must be reported to IT. If IT confirms that a repair or replacement will take more than one working day, IT may authorize a coworking pass or temporary device. Employees may not purchase a personal computer and seek reimbursement.

## 11. Leave interaction

Attendance-based allowances are suspended during paid caregiver leave. Monthly internet support remains available only for a month in which the employee performs at least one working day. Short-term remote work during medical recovery requires manager approval; arrangements longer than 5 working days may require a medical note under the Leave and Attendance Policy.

## 12. Approval matrix

| Expense | Required approval |
|---|---|
| Home-office claim up to ₹25,000 | Manager + Finance validation |
| Coworking pass | Manager |
| Learning expense up to ₹20,000 | Manager |
| Learning expense above ₹20,000 | Manager + department head |
| Late claim, 31–60 days | Department head |
| Accessibility accommodation | People Operations written approval |
| International remote work | Legal + Information Security + People Operations |

## 13. Fraud, duplicate claims, and repayment

Duplicate, altered, or intentionally misleading claims may lead to repayment and disciplinary review. Accidental duplicate claims should be reported promptly. Finance may offset confirmed overpayments against future reimbursements where lawful and with notice.

## 14. Examples

### Example A: Chair and monitor

A Hybrid employee buys a ₹14,000 chair and a ₹17,000 monitor. Both are below their individual caps, but the combined eligible total is limited to ₹25,000 by the overall setup allowance.

### Example B: Late internet claim

A Remote employee submits a ₹1,400 broadband bill 40 days after payment. The amount is within the monthly cap, but department-head approval is required because the claim is 31–60 days late.

### Example C: Leave for a full month

An employee is on paid caregiver leave for all of August and performs no work. The employee cannot claim August internet support because there was no worked day, even though health coverage continues under the benefits policy.

## 15. Policy silence

Finance will not infer eligibility from similarity to a listed item. If an expense is not addressed, request written pre-approval before purchase.
