# Northstar Dynamics Health and Wellbeing Benefits Policy

**Policy ID:** BEN-HW-2026  
**Version:** 2.4  
**Effective date:** 1 July 2026  
**Policy owner:** Total Rewards  
**Insurer:** Meridian Assurance (fictional)

> This fictional policy is designed for RAG evaluation. Coverage is subject to the definitions, limits, waiting periods, and exclusions below.

## 1. Eligibility and enrollment

Permanent employees are eligible from their employment start date. A spouse or domestic partner and up to two dependent children under age 25 may be enrolled. Parents are available only through the optional Parent Add-on. New dependants must be enrolled within 30 calendar days of the qualifying life event.

Employees choose one of three tiers during enrollment: Standard, Enhanced, or Premier. A tier normally remains fixed until the next annual enrollment window unless a qualifying life event occurs.

## 2. Annual coverage limits

| Benefit | Standard | Enhanced | Premier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| In-patient hospitalization | ₹5,00,000 family floater | ₹10,00,000 family floater | ₹20,00,000 family floater |
| Out-patient consultations | ₹8,000 | ₹20,000 | ₹40,000 |
| Diagnostic tests | ₹10,000 | ₹25,000 | ₹50,000 |
| Mental-health sessions | 6 sessions | 12 sessions | 20 sessions |
| Dental routine care | ₹3,000 | ₹8,000 | ₹15,000 |

| Physiotherapy | 8 sessions | 15 sessions | 25 sessions |

All rupee limits are per family per policy year unless stated otherwise. Session limits are per covered person per policy year.

## 3. Hospitalization

### 3.1 Room rent

Standard covers a shared room up to ₹5,000 per day. Enhanced covers a private room up to ₹10,000 per day. Premier covers a private room with no separate room-rent sublimit, subject to the overall hospitalization limit.

### 3.2 Pre- and post-hospitalization

Medically necessary expenses are covered for 30 days before and 60 days after hospitalization under Standard; 45 days before and 90 days after under Enhanced; and 60 days before and 120 days after under Premier.

### 3.3 Cashless and reimbursement claims

Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals after pre-authorization, except emergencies. Reimbursement claims must be filed within 30 calendar days after discharge and include the discharge summary, itemized bills, prescriptions, and payment receipts.

## 4. Dental benefits

### 4.1 Routine dental care

Routine examination, cleaning, simple fillings, and medically necessary extraction are covered up to the dental annual limit shown in Section 2.

### 4.2 Dental implants

| Tier | Dental implant coverage |
|---|---|
| Standard | Not covered |
| Enhanced | 50% of eligible cost, maximum ₹25,000 per implant, one implant per policy year |
| Premier | 80% of eligible cost, maximum ₹60,000 per implant, two implants per policy year |

Implants required solely for cosmetic appearance are excluded in every tier. Pre-authorization is mandatory for Enhanced and Premier. Bone grafting is covered only when clinically required for an approved implant and counts toward the same per-implant cap.

### 4.3 Orthodontics

Orthodontics is excluded under Standard and Enhanced. Premier covers medically necessary orthodontics for dependent children under age 18, up to a lifetime maximum of ₹75,000 per child. Cosmetic alignment is excluded.

## 5. Mental health and neurodevelopmental care

Covered mental-health sessions may be delivered in person or by video through a licensed provider. Psychiatric medication is covered under out-patient limits when prescribed. Learning assessments requested only by a school are not covered; clinically indicated neurodevelopmental assessments are covered under diagnostic-test limits after referral by a pediatrician or psychiatrist.

The Employee Assistance Programme provides an additional 4 confidential counseling sessions per issue. EAP sessions do not consume insurance session limits and are available to all employees, including employees on probation.

## 6. Preventive care

One annual preventive health check is covered for the employee and enrolled spouse or domestic partner. Standard uses designated packages at network clinics. Enhanced reimburses up to ₹4,000 per person. Premier reimburses up to ₹8,000 per person. Preventive checks do not reduce diagnostic-test limits.

Vaccinations listed in the annual preventive schedule are covered at network providers. Travel vaccinations are covered only when business travel is authorized under the Travel Policy.

## 7. Maternity, adoption, and newborn care

Maternity hospitalization is covered within the family hospitalization limit after a 9-month waiting period, waived when the employee was continuously covered by a previous employer plan immediately before joining. Newborn medical expenses are covered from birth if enrollment is completed within 30 calendar days. Leave duration and salary continuation are controlled by the Leave and Attendance Policy, not this policy.

## 8. Physiotherapy and rehabilitation

Physiotherapy requires a referral from a registered medical practitioner. The session limits in Section 2 apply. Post-operative rehabilitation received within the applicable post-hospitalization window is charged to hospitalization coverage rather than the standalone physiotherapy session limit when the insurer approves it as part of the same claim.

## 9. Wellbeing wallet

Each permanent employee receives a **₹12,000 annual wellbeing wallet**, prorated monthly in the first year. The wallet is separate from insurance and reimbursements are governed by eligible categories below.

### 9.1 Eligible wellbeing expenses

- Gym, yoga, swimming, or fitness-class membership.
- Fitness tracker, capped at ₹6,000 once every two policy years.
- Meditation or sleep application subscription.
- Entry fees for organized running, cycling, or swimming events.
- Ergonomic assessment prescribed by an occupational therapist.

### 9.2 Ineligible wellbeing expenses

- Purchase of home-gym machines, weights, treadmills, or exercise bicycles.
- Sports apparel or footwear.
- Supplements, vitamins, protein powders, or meal plans.
- Spa, beauty, or cosmetic services.
- Equipment purchased from a family member or unregistered seller.

A gym membership used at home or online is eligible, but **personal home-gym equipment is not eligible**. Claims require an invoice and proof of payment and must be filed within 45 calendar days of purchase.

## 10. Major exclusions

The plan does not cover treatment that is unlawful, experimental treatment without regulator approval, non-medically indicated cosmetic procedures, injuries arising from professional competitive sports, or fertility preservation for non-medical convenience. Emergency stabilization is covered even when the underlying event may later be subject to an exclusion, as required by applicable law and insurer rules.

## 11. Coordination with leave and workplace benefits

Medical coverage continues during paid caregiver leave and approved unpaid leave of up to 90 calendar days. After 90 days of unpaid leave, the employee must pay the full monthly premium to continue coverage. Home-office furniture and internet expenses are not health-plan benefits; consult the Workplace and Expense Policy.

## 12. Claims escalation

Employees should first raise a claim query with the benefits helpdesk. If unresolved after 7 working days, submit an appeal to Total Rewards with the claim number and supporting documents. A final insurer decision does not alter leave eligibility.

## 13. Examples

### Example A: Enhanced dental implant

An eligible implant costs ₹70,000. Enhanced covers 50%, which is ₹35,000, but the per-implant maximum is ₹25,000. The payable amount is therefore ₹25,000, subject to pre-authorization and annual frequency limits.

### Example B: Home fitness

A ₹9,000 online yoga membership is eligible within the ₹12,000 wallet. A ₹9,000 set of personal weights is not eligible because home-gym equipment is expressly excluded.

### Example C: Rehabilitation after surgery

Ten approved physiotherapy sessions within Enhanced's 90-day post-hospitalization window may be charged to the hospitalization claim and need not consume the 15-session standalone limit.

## 14. Contact and policy silence

For coverage not stated here, employees must not assume it is covered. Contact the benefits helpdesk for a written determination.
