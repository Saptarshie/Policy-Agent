# Northstar Dynamics Leave and Attendance Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-LA-2026  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Effective date:** 1 April 2026  
**Policy owner:** People Operations  
**Applies to:** India-based permanent employees unless a section states otherwise

> This is a fictional policy created for testing retrieval-augmented generation systems. Where this policy is silent, employees must contact People Operations.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines leave entitlements, carry-forward rules, attendance expectations, approval paths, and interactions between leave and other employee programmes. Interns, contractors, consultants, and employees serving notice periods are covered only where explicitly stated.

## 2. Definitions

- **Calendar year:** 1 January through 31 December.
- **Working day:** A scheduled workday for the employee's assigned location, excluding declared company holidays.
- **Continuous leave:** Consecutive scheduled working days charged to the same leave category.
- **Primary caregiver:** The parent or legal guardian taking the principal day-to-day caregiving role following birth, adoption, or surrogacy.
- **Secondary caregiver:** An eligible parent or guardian who is not designated as the primary caregiver.
- **Probation:** The initial employment period stated in the employment letter, normally six months.

## 4. Casual Leave

### 4.1 Accrual and use

Permanent employees receive 12 days of Casual Leave per calendar year, credited monthly at 1 day per completed month. A newly hired employee begins accruing from the employment start date. Casual Leave may be used in half-day or full-day units.

### 4.2 Carry-forward

A maximum of **5 unused Casual Leave days** may be carried into the next calendar year. The total Casual Leave balance may not exceed **17 days** after the annual credit. Any amount above 5 unused days at year-end expires automatically and is not encashed.

### 4.3 Probation restriction

Employees on probation may use accrued Casual Leave but may not take more than 3 consecutive Casual Leave days without approval from both the manager and People Operations.

## 5. Earned Leave

### 5.1 Accrual

Permanent employees receive 18 days of Earned Leave annually, accrued at 1.5 days per completed month.

### 5.2 Carry-forward and encashment

Up to 30 unused Earned Leave days carry forward. The balance cap is 48 days. Balances above 48 days are automatically encashed in the March payroll at the employee's basic daily salary rate, except during a notice period.

### 5.3 Advance notice

Requests for 4 or more consecutive Earned Leave days should be submitted at least 10 calendar days in advance. Emergency exceptions require manager and People Operations approval.

## 6. Sick and wellness leave

### 6.1 Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 Sick Leave days each calendar year. Sick Leave does not carry forward and is not encashed. A medical certificate is required when Sick Leave exceeds 2 consecutive working days. For hospitalization, the employee or emergency contact should notify the manager as soon as practical.

### 6.2 Wellness Leave

Two full Wellness Leave days are available annually. They cannot be split into half-days, carried forward, or encashed. Employees need not disclose a diagnosis or reason. Wellness Leave cannot be attached directly before or after Earned Leave of 4 or more consecutive working days unless People Operations approves an exception.

## 7. Caregiver leave

### 7.1 Primary caregiver leave

An eligible primary caregiver receives 26 weeks of paid leave. Eligibility begins after 80 calendar days of continuous employment. Leave must commence no earlier than 8 weeks before the expected event date and must be completed within 12 months after birth, adoption, or surrogacy placement.

### 7.2 Secondary caregiver leave

An eligible secondary caregiver receives 20 paid working days, to be used within 6 months of birth, adoption, or surrogacy placement. It may be taken in no more than two blocks, each of at least 5 working days.

### 7.3 Benefit interaction

During paid caregiver leave, base salary and employer health coverage continue. Meal allowance and commuting allowance are suspended because they are attendance-based. Home-office reimbursements are governed by the Workplace and Expense Policy.

## 8. Bereavement and family emergency

Employees may take:

- 5 paid working days for the death of a spouse, child, parent, parent-in-law, or legal guardian.
- 3 paid working days for the death of a sibling or grandparent.
- Up to 2 paid working days per calendar year for an urgent family emergency, subject to manager notification.

Bereavement leave does not reduce Casual, Earned, or Sick Leave balances.

## 9. Holidays and weekends during leave

Declared company holidays and weekly rest days falling inside a continuous Earned Leave block are not deducted from the leave balance. For Casual Leave, only scheduled working days are deducted. A location-specific holiday applies according to the employee's assigned work location in HR records, not the location from which the employee happens to work that day.

## 10. Remote work during recovery

Remote work is not a substitute for Sick Leave. An employee who is fit to work but temporarily unable to commute may request short-term remote work under the Workplace and Expense Policy. A medical note may be requested for arrangements longer than 5 working days.

## 11. Notice period and separation

During a notice period, employees may use accrued Sick Leave for genuine illness and up to 2 Casual Leave days with manager approval. Earned Leave is normally encashed at separation rather than taken, unless the business head approves otherwise. Unused Casual, Sick, Wellness, Volunteer, and caregiver leave is not encashed.

## 12. Request and escalation process

Employees submit planned leave in the HR portal. Managers should respond within 3 working days. If a request remains unanswered after 3 working days, the employee may escalate to People Operations. Sick Leave and urgent family emergency leave may be recorded retrospectively within 2 working days after return.

## 13. Examples

### Example A: Casual Leave carry-forward

An employee ends 2026 with 8 unused Casual Leave days. Five days carry into 2027 and three expire. After the 2027 annual entitlement is credited over the year, the balance may never exceed 17 days.

### Example B: Long illness

An employee takes Monday through Wednesday as Sick Leave. Because this exceeds 2 consecutive working days, a medical certificate is required.

### Example C: Location holiday

An employee assigned to Guwahati works remotely from Kolkata during a Guwahati location holiday. The Guwahati holiday calendar applies because the HR-assigned location controls.

## 14. Exclusions and contact

This policy does not define insurance coverage, expense reimbursement amounts, travel class, retirement benefits, or disciplinary procedures. Contact People Operations when the policy does not answer a question.
