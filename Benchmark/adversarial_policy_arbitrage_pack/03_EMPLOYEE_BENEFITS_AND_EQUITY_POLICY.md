# Novacrest Group Employee Benefits and Equity Policy

**Policy ID:** NG-BEN-2032  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2032  
**Employer:** Novacrest Group, a fictional employer

> Synthetic policy for adversarial benchmark testing. It includes benefit coordination, ownership attribution, policy anti-abuse rules, repayment triggers, and narrow safe harbors.

## 1. Eligibility and household aggregation

Permanent employees working at least 30 hours weekly are eligible unless a benefit states otherwise. For benefits with household limits, household includes the employee, spouse or domestic partner, and dependent children. Reimbursement through another household member, insurer, government program, or company plan must be disclosed.

## 2. Professional Development Account

Each employee receives N$150,000 per calendar year for role-relevant courses, examinations, books, and professional memberships.

Pre-approval is required for a single commitment above N$40,000. Reimbursement is based on net cost after scholarships, discounts, refunds, employer sponsorship, and transferable credits.

The account cannot fund degree tuition, equipment, travel unrelated to an approved course, or services supplied by the employee or a related person. Employees may not divide one course into modules, invoices, or payment plans to avoid pre-approval.

If an employee voluntarily resigns within six months after an individual reimbursement above N$80,000, 50% is repayable. Multiple reimbursements arising from one enrollment agreement are aggregated.

## 3. Wellness and accessibility

### 3.1 Wellness wallet

Employees receive N$36,000 yearly for gym memberships, sports participation, meditation services, and preventive wellbeing programs. Equipment, supplements, medical treatment, and cash-equivalent gift cards are excluded.

### 3.2 Accessibility accommodation

Reasonable accessibility accommodations are funded separately without a fixed individual cap after approval by the Accommodation Committee. The accommodation must address a documented functional limitation and be suitable for work. Personal convenience, general wellness, and items primarily used by other household members do not qualify.

A wellness exclusion cannot be avoided merely by describing an item as ergonomic. However, the same category of item may qualify as an accommodation when the individualized process independently establishes necessity.

## 4. Commuter and remote-work support

Office employees receive actual public-transport cost up to N$6,000 monthly. Hybrid employees receive either commuter support or remote-work support for a given month, not both. Remote-work support is N$4,000 per month where the employee works remotely at least eight scheduled days.

A day split between home and office counts according to where more scheduled hours were worked. Business travel days count as neither. Employees may choose the more favorable benefit after month-end based on actual facts, but may not alter attendance records or defer claims across months.

## 5. Dependent-care benefit

The company reimburses 50% of eligible dependent-care cost, capped at N$180,000 per household per year. Care must enable the employee or spouse to work, seek work, or attend full-time education.

Payments to the employee's dependent child, spouse, domestic partner, or the child's parent are excluded. Payments to another related person are eligible only if the provider operates a registered care business serving at least five unrelated families and charges the employee no less than its ordinary public rate.

If both spouses work for Novacrest, the household has one shared cap, not two. Claims may be allocated between spouses to use payroll or timing advantages, but combined reimbursement cannot exceed the household cap or 50% of net cost.

## 6. Referral awards

An employee receives N$50,000 when a referred candidate completes six months of service. No award is available where the employee participates in the hiring decision, the candidate reported to the employee during the prior two years, or the referral was first submitted by another person.

Employees cannot route a referral through a colleague in exchange for sharing the award. A colleague who independently knew and referred the candidate without an agreement remains eligible.

## 7. Innovation awards

The company may pay N$100,000 for an implemented suggestion producing verified annual savings above N$1,000,000. Suggestions developed as an assigned job duty qualify only for team recognition, not cash, unless the Innovation Committee issued a written challenge open to all employees.

Splitting one idea among employees or submitting incremental restatements does not create multiple awards. Distinct improvements may each qualify if independently implemented and each produces separately verified savings.

## 8. Equity plan

### 8.1 Options

Eligible employees may receive options that vest 25% annually over four years. Upon voluntary resignation, vested options remain exercisable for 90 days; unvested options lapse. Approved retirement after age 60 extends exercise to one year.

### 8.2 Change in control

If a change in control occurs and the employee is terminated without cause or resigns for Good Reason within 12 months, all then-unvested options accelerate. Good Reason requires a material salary reduction, relocation over 75 km, or material reduction in authority, followed by written notice within 30 days and a 30-day employer cure opportunity.

### 8.3 Transfers

Options cannot be sold or transferred except to the employee's estate on death. Any agreement to share option proceeds with another person does not transfer plan rights, but may create separate obligations and does not bind the company.

### 8.4 Exercise funding

The company may permit same-day sale to fund exercise. A personal loan from another employee is not prohibited, but bribery, kickback, undisclosed conflict, or repayment contingent on workplace decisions violates the Code of Conduct.

## 9. Business expense interaction

An expense cannot be claimed from both this policy and the Business Expense Policy. If an expense could fit multiple categories, the most specific benefit applies. A rejected claim cannot be resubmitted under a less specific category unless new facts establish independent eligibility.

## 10. Anti-abuse rule

Benefits may be denied or recovered where transactions are artificial, circular, collusive, falsely documented, or principally arranged to obtain a payment inconsistent with the benefit's purpose. Selecting a more favorable option expressly allowed by the policy is not abuse.

The company must identify the inconsistent purpose and relevant facts. An employee who fully discloses a novel arrangement and receives written pre-clearance from Benefits Compliance may rely on that approval unless material facts were omitted.

## 11. Claims and appeals

Claims are due within 45 days after payment unless a section states otherwise. Denials may be appealed within 30 days. Benefits Compliance decisions must state the policy basis. Payroll errors may be corrected within two years, except deliberate fraud has no time limit.
