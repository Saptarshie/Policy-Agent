# Republic of Norland Enterprise Incentives and Grants Act 2032

**Instrument:** EIGA-2032  
**Effective date:** 1 April 2032  
**Administrator:** Norland Enterprise Agency

> Entirely fictional. This instrument tests eligibility chaining, aggregation rules, clawbacks, timing, and attempts to exploit programme boundaries.

## 1. Programs

The Act establishes the Green Equipment Rebate, Regional Employment Credit, Innovation Matching Grant, and Export Readiness Voucher. A claimant may use multiple programs only where Section 8 permits stacking.

## 2. Common control

Entities are under common control when the same person or related persons directly or indirectly control more than 50% of voting rights, economic rights, board appointment, or practical decision-making. Options, nominee holdings, side agreements, and coordinated action are considered.

Businesses under common control are treated as one enterprise for employee thresholds, annual caps, project-cost thresholds, and connected-project rules, unless a program expressly states otherwise.

## 3. Green Equipment Rebate

### 3.1 Benefit

A qualifying enterprise receives 25% of eligible cost for new energy-efficient production equipment, capped at N$2,000,000 per enterprise group per fiscal year.

### 3.2 Eligibility

Equipment must be installed in Norland, used primarily in an active trade, remain in qualifying use for three years, and replace or materially reduce consumption compared with existing operations.

### 3.3 Exclusions

Passenger vehicles, buildings, used equipment, equipment acquired from a related person, and equipment mainly leased to another person are excluded. A sale-and-leaseback completed within 12 months is treated according to economic ownership.

### 3.4 Connected acquisitions

Orders, invoices, entities, and installations forming part of one commercial project are aggregated. A project cannot be fragmented across subsidiaries or fiscal years to multiply caps where binding commitments or a unified implementation plan existed before the split.

A genuine later expansion responding to unanticipated demand is separate if approved by the board after the first project became operational and neither party was committed to it earlier.

## 4. Regional Employment Credit

### 4.1 Benefit

A credit of N$120,000 per qualifying net new full-time job is available in a designated region, capped at N$3,600,000 per enterprise group per year.

### 4.2 Qualifying job

A job qualifies if it:

- averages at least 30 hours per week;
- is located and ordinarily performed in the designated region;
- is maintained for at least 18 months; and
- increases the group's average full-time headcount compared with the preceding 12 months.

### 4.3 Transfers and replacements

A role transferred from elsewhere in the group is not net new. Replacing an employee who left within the previous six months is not net new unless the earlier role was eliminated for a documented reason unrelated to the credit and the new role has materially different functions.

Two part-time roles cannot be combined unless each averages at least 15 hours weekly, neither worker is related to an owner, and the arrangement reflects genuine operational scheduling rather than credit qualification.

### 4.4 Clawback

If a credited job ends before 18 months, the credit is repaid proportionally, except for employee death, permanent disability, or voluntary resignation followed by a replacement within 60 days.

## 5. Innovation Matching Grant

### 5.1 Benefit

The Agency matches 40% of eligible research expenditure, up to N$5,000,000 per connected project. Payment occurs after verified expenditure.

### 5.2 Eligible research

Eligible work must seek a scientific or technological advance by resolving uncertainty not readily solvable by a competent professional. Routine customization, market research, cosmetic design, ordinary debugging, and duplication are excluded.

### 5.3 Related-party research

Payments to a related research supplier are eligible only at the lower of actual cost plus 10% or the arm's-length price. The supplier must maintain records of staff, materials, and work performed. Unsupported management fees are excluded.

### 5.4 Background intellectual property

Purchase of pre-existing IP is not eligible research expenditure. Licensing cost may qualify only to the extent indispensable to eligible experiments and limited to the project's period.

## 6. Export Readiness Voucher

A small enterprise may receive up to N$500,000 for foreign certification, localization, export compliance, and market-entry advisory services. An enterprise ceases to be small if its aggregated group revenue exceeds N$100,000,000 in the prior fiscal year.

Payments to owners, employees, related persons, or entities under common control are excluded. A provider is related where an owner has a direct or indirect economic interest, including a contingent referral interest.

## 7. Application integrity

Applicants must disclose common control, related parties, prior commitments, side agreements, and other public funding. A technically true statement is misleading if it omits a fact necessary to prevent the statement from creating a false impression.

An eligibility opinion from a consultant does not bind the Agency. Written pre-clearance binds the Agency only on fully and accurately disclosed facts.

## 8. Stacking and double funding

### 8.1 Permitted stacking

Different programs may support the same broader business initiative only if they fund distinct costs and each cost satisfies its program.

### 8.2 Prohibited duplication

The same invoice, economic cost, employee time, equipment component, or obligation cannot support more than one public benefit. A cost reimbursed by insurance, a supplier credit, litigation recovery, or another grant must be reduced before benefit calculation.

### 8.3 Tax interaction

A grant reduces the tax basis or deductible expenditure of the supported item unless the Revenue Code expressly provides otherwise. The Enterprise Agency does not determine the tax treatment.

## 9. Change of ownership

A recipient must notify the Agency before a change of control during a benefit maintenance period. Benefits survive if qualifying operations and obligations continue and the acquirer accepts them. A transaction structured principally to sell access to unused program caps may be denied or clawed back.

## 10. Anti-fragmentation and principal-purpose test

The Agency may aggregate or disregard arrangements when a principal purpose is obtaining a benefit unavailable to the enterprise as a whole and the arrangement lacks independent commercial substance. Indicators include nominal directors, circular payments, temporary ownership, mirrored contracts, shared staff with no allocation, and entities that cannot perform their stated functions.

A separately managed business line with distinct customers, staff, assets, risks, and financing is not aggregated merely because it shares a parent, although common-control caps still apply where expressly stated.

## 11. Recovery and penalties

Incorrect benefits must be repaid with benchmark interest. A 20% penalty applies to careless material misstatements and 60% to deliberate concealment. No penalty applies to a fully disclosed position based on reasonable written interpretation, but repayment still applies.
